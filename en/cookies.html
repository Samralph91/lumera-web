Cookies

Information about the use of cookies and similar technologies on lumera.sk. Effective from 1 November 2026.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They help the site work or remember your settings.

2. Which cookies our website uses

Our website itself uses no analytics, marketing or advertising cookies and does not track you. The pages display without any cookies.

Cookies may only be stored by a third-party service shown on the website:

ServicePurposeWhere it appears
Google Maps
Google Ireland Ltd.		Displays a map with the location of our office. Google may store its own cookies under its policies.On pages with the map (home, contact and others).

3. Other services without cookies

  • Google Fonts – loads the fonts. Your IP address is passed to Google in the process, but no cookies are stored.
  • FormSubmit – delivers contact forms to our e-mail. It only processes the data you enter in the form yourself.

4. How to change your settings

You can delete or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. If you block third-party cookies, the map may display with limited functionality.

5. More information

You can read about how we process personal data in our Privacy policy. Send any questions to info@lumera.sk.
