Property description

For sale: a sunny, flat 770 m² building plot in Zamarovce, at the end of the quiet Pod Kopánky cul-de-sac – away from through traffic and close to Trenčín. The plot is fenced, has an entrance gate and mature thujas along the rear boundary.

Its elongated shape, approx. 14 m wide, lets you separate the entrance area from a private living zone and garden. There is a private well (approx. 16 m) and 230 V electricity on the plot; according to the documents, gas is nearby. There is no sewer connection; waste water goes to a septic tank. Part of the access road is included in the sale.

Under the zoning plan the plot is designated for a family house with max. 35 % coverage and up to two storeys. Architectural drawings for a family house with a garage, on which the visualisations are based, are available. There is a kindergarten nearby.

The visualisations are for illustration only. Only the plot is for sale – the house, garage, pool and landscaping are not included in the price.
