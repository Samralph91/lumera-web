WHAT WE DO FOR YOU

A complete service – from pricing to handing over the keys

Selling or letting a property takes a lot of time and nerves. We take care of everything and keep you informed at every step.

01

A price based on the real market

We set the price based on properties actually sold in your area, not on guesswork. The right price from day one shortens the sale and protects you from unnecessary discounts.

02

Photos, drone and visualisations

We photograph your property so it stands out, including drone shots. For flats needing renovation and for plots we prepare visualisations that show buyers what they could become.

03

Advertising that sells

We publish your listing on the largest property portals and on our website – with copy that captures what matters, aimed at exactly the people looking for a property like yours.

04

Home staging

Buyers decide with their eyes and emotions. We prepare and furnish your property to appeal to as many buyers as possible and feel like a home rather than an empty space. We only remove the props after a successful sale.

05

Viewings to remember

We prepare the property and refreshments for each viewing so visitors feel welcome and have time to look around calmly. We screen interested buyers in advance, conduct the viewings ourselves and negotiate the best terms for you.

06

Contracts and paperwork handled for you

Reservation and purchase agreements, title deed checks, documents for the bank and the land registry application. We handle it all – without you having to take time off work.

07

A mortgage for the buyer

We help the buyer with financing in-house, so the deal doesn't stall waiting for the bank and the money reaches your account sooner.
